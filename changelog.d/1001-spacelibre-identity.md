- The site wears the spacelibre identity. The official lockup from the visual
  identity manual is used as one unmodified vector (symbol and wordmark
  together, geometry untouched), recoloured from the active palette instead of
  being restyled, filtered or split. The icon and favicon are the manual's
  monochrome white symbol.
- Colour is a setting, not a constant, as the manual specifies: six presets plus
  any custom primary, with every role derived from it. The mark, the HUD, the
  first-run mission launcher and the panels all read the same tokens, so a
  chosen scheme applies everywhere at once. `?primary=RRGGBB&mode=…` previews a
  scheme without storing it.
- The palette is resolved before the first frame by a tiny blocking script, so
  the loading screen already appears in the stored scheme instead of flashing
  the default and correcting itself after boot.
- The old God's Eye title text, eye logo, its pointer-following animation and
  the Cyber theme's per-part recolouring of it are gone.
